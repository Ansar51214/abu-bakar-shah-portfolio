About

Profile of Public Education Service

Muhammad Abu Bakar Siddique Shah serves as District Education Officer (Literacy), bringing together academic training in English, classroom experience, and administrative responsibility for literacy-oriented education work. His professional focus is grounded in improving access, strengthening teaching practice, and supporting programs that help learners build functional reading, writing, and communication skills.

With an MPhil in English, his work connects language education with practical public-sector delivery: planning literacy initiatives, guiding teachers and field teams, reviewing academic progress, and encouraging a culture of disciplined research, documentation, and measurable educational improvement.
